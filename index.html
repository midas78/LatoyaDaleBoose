Reflections

Latoya, affectionately known as Toya or Toy, was born on February 2, 1977, in Forrest City, Arkansas. She was the oldest of her parents’ three children. As the eldest, Toya never allowed her younger siblings, Johnny Jr. and Tamika, to forget that she was firstborn—and, in her view, knew more than both of them.

Even at the age of four, Latoya’s intellect and confidence were unmistakable. She had learned all the basics before kindergarten and decided to recite her alphabet backward for extra applause. It was not long before Johnny Jr. and Tamika began to believe she knew more than their parents. Whenever their parents answered a question, the siblings often followed with the same phrase: “I am going to ask Toy.”

Latoya excelled at everything she approached. She graduated from Forrest City High School in 1995, ranking in the top ten percent of her class. She continued her education at Philander Smith College in Little Rock, Arkansas, and later earned a Bachelor of Science in Business Management from Capella University and a Master of Science in Management and Leadership from Western Governors University. Her academic achievements reflected a lifelong commitment to learning, growth, and leadership.

Her professional journey began at McDonald’s during high school and later expanded into warehouse positions following college. She soon realized she needed to be the boss, so she pursued and worked in management in fast food, truck stops, retail clothing, and Amazon. Her career was marked by ambition, versatility, and a strong desire to lead.

Latoya lived in Memphis for more than twenty years before deciding to move to Atlanta. She loved living in Atlanta and exploring all the options the city offered—not only for church, but also for dining, sports activities, and meeting and sharing spaces with actors and other celebrities.

She leaves to cherish her memory her siblings, Johnny Jr. and Tamika; her parents, Johnny Sr. and Wanda; and a host of relatives and friends. She will be remembered for her sharp mind, her bold spirit, and the love and leadership she brought to her family.
